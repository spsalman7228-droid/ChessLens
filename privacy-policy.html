1. Who We Are and What ChessLens Does

ChessLens is a visual chess learning and analysis application designed to assist users in understanding chess positions from video streams, recordings, diagrams, screenshots, puzzle apps, and physical boards.

2. Intended Use and Fair Play Policy

ChessLens is strictly intended for study, education, post-game review, puzzle training, and video analysis. It is NOT designed or intended to be used during live, rated, competitive, tournament, or rule-restricted games against other human players. Using ChessLens for real-time assistance during rated online or offline games violates our Terms of Use and the fair-play policies of chess platforms.

3. Accessibility Service Disclosure

ChessLens utilizes the Android Accessibility API (AccessibilityService) under explicit user authorization to detect chessboards and draw instructional arrows. The service is active only in user-allowlisted apps and runs entirely on-device.

4. On-Device Processing Only

Screen pixels and video frames are read directly into device RAM and processed in real time. They are never stored, saved, or uploaded. Optional FEN history is kept locally on device.
